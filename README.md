# Enchanted Lucky Blocks  
Introducing a new Minecraft mod: Enchanted Lucky Blocks! There are two types of these blocks: green and yellow. The green blocks are similar to normal lucky blocks and offer resources and effects, while the yellow blocks present a challenge: if you fail, you will lose 3 health points (6 hearts) permanently.  

On the top left corner of each block, there is a lucky coin, which is the currency in the lucky block universe. Breaking a green lucky block gives you +3 lucky coins, while a yellow lucky block grants you +9 lucky coins.  
In your inventory, there is also a leveling menu where you can use your lucky coins to improve your health, armour, and luck, as well as unique perks such as being able to fly without elytras.  
Good luck on your journey with the lucky blocks!
